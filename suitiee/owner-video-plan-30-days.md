# Suitiee: 30-Day Owner Acquisition Video Plan (TikTok + Instagram Reels)

**Owner:** CMO, Suitiee (suitiee.com, @suitiee.sa)
**Market:** Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
**Audience:** Property **owners** (not guests)
**Format:** Organic short video, built so every video can also run as a Spark Ad / Meta ad without re-shooting
**Presenter:** Saudi male, white Saudi dialect (اللهجة السعودية البيضاء)
**Volume:** 14 videos over 30 days (3–4 per week)
**Paid budget:** 3,000–10,000 SAR (baseline plan: 6,000 SAR)

## 0. The one-line strategy

> **Show Riyadh owners the gap between what their apartment earns now and what it could earn with Suitiee, prove it with our own numbers and behind-the-scenes footage, and turn every video into a lead with one comment keyword.**

The structure copies the lead-magnet format from the reference files:
**big promise → numbered steps → one key insight → one clear action.**
The reference doc says *"The important part isn't the 2,000 transcripts. It's the combination…"*. That reframe line is the most shareable part of the format, so every script here has one.

## 5. Algorithm playbook: TikTok + Instagram Reels

### What both platforms reward
1. **The first 1–2 seconds.** A visual hook, on-screen text and the spoken hook all start at frame 1. No logo intro, no "السلام عليكم، معكم…".
2. **Watch time and completion.** Target 30–50 seconds. Cut every pause and breath (jump cuts every 2–4 s).
3. **Rewatches and loops.** End on a line that connects back to the opening, or on a number that makes people scroll back.
4. **Comments.** The keyword CTA is also an algorithm signal: every "تم" is a comment.
5. **Shares/sends.** On Instagram, **sends per reach** is one of the strongest distribution signals. Give owners something to forward to a brother or partner who owns a flat ("أرسلها لأخوك اللي عنده شقة").
6. **Search (SEO).** TikTok is used as a search engine in KSA. Say the keywords out loud (auto-captions are indexed), put them in the on-screen text **and** the caption: *إدارة شقق الرياض، تأجير يومي، جاذر إن، إير بي إن بي، دخل الشقة، إيجار سنوي*.

### TikTok specifics
- Post natively from the TikTok app/Business Suite. Use a **trending but low-volume sound** under the voice (10–15% volume) on organic posts. Ad versions use **Commercial Music Library** sounds only.
- 3–5 hashtags: 1 broad (#الرياض), 2 niche (#تأجير_يومي #إدارة_أملاك), 1 brand (#سويتي).
- Pin a comment with the keyword instruction on every video.
- **Reply to comments with video** (V12 is built around this). TikTok pushes these to people who engaged before.
- Don't delete low performers in the first 72h. TikTok often re-surfaces videos days later.

### Instagram Reels specifics
- **Never upload the TikTok file with the watermark.** Export the clean master from CapCut/editor and upload separately. Instagram suppresses recycled, watermarked content.
- Use **Trial Reels** for hook tests: the same body with 2 different hooks is shown to non-followers first.
- Keep on-screen text out of the bottom 20% and the right 15% (UI overlap).
- Caption: first line = hook restated + keyword CTA; 3–5 hashtags.
- Share every Reel to Stories with a poll sticker ("عندك شقة مؤجرة سنوي؟ نعم / لا"). The poll answers give you a retargeting audience.

### Posting schedule (Riyadh time, starting point: validate with your analytics in week 2)
| Day | Slot | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Sun / Tue / Thu | 8:30–10:00 PM | After Isha, peak evening scroll |
| Sat | 1:00–3:00 PM | Weekend midday, owners have time to think about money |

This mirrors the reference file: **Promise → numbered steps → key insight → action.**

| Time | Block | What happens | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | **HOOK** | Pattern interrupt + on-screen text + spoken line | Must name the owner or their money in the first sentence |
| 2–7s | **PROBLEM / PROMISE** | The pain the owner already feels + what they'll get by staying | One sentence each |
| 7–35s | **3 STEPS / 3 POINTS** | Numbered on screen: ١ / ٢ / ٣ | B-roll changes on every number |
| 35–42s | **KEY INSIGHT (the reframe)** | "المهم مو …، المهم …" | The shareable line |
| 42–50s | **CTA** | "اكتب **{keyword}** بالتعليقات وأرسل لك التفاصيل على الخاص" | Keyword also on screen, big |

**Ad-ready rules baked into every shoot:**
- Film **3 alternative hooks** per video (different first sentence, same body). These become the A/B/C ad variants.
- Film the CTA twice: once with **"اكتب تم بالتعليقات"** (organic) and once with **"اضغط الزر تحت وكلمنا واتساب"** (ads, where comments aren't the conversion path).
- Keep 5–10 seconds of clean B-roll per video (apartment, keys, app screen) for ad edits.
- Burn in Arabic subtitles (most people watch muted), in the white Saudi dialect exactly as spoken, **not** MSA.

## 7. Arabic language guide: "make sure it doesn't look odd"

The biggest risk is a script that sounds translated or read off a page. Rules:

**Do:**
- Talk like you'd explain it to a cousin at a jalsa: short sentences, one idea each.
- Use natural fillers sparingly: *بصراحة، شوف، خلني أقولك، والله، يعني*.
- Round numbers in speech: "قرابة ٧٠ ألف" rather than "٦٩٬٨٤٠ ريال".
- Platform names as people say them: **إير بي إن بي، جاذر إن، بوكينق**.
- Film in real places: a managed apartment, the car outside a building, the ops room. No studio.

**Don't:**
| ❌ Sounds odd / translated | ✅ Say instead |
|---|---|
| هل تمتلك شقة؟ | عندك شقة؟ |
| نحن نقدم خدمات إدارة العقارات | إحنا نشيل عنك الشقة من الألف للياء |
| قم بكتابة كلمة تم | اكتب "تم" بالتعليقات |
| سوف تحصل على دخل أعلى | دخلك بيزيد، وبالأرقام |
| عزيزي المالك | يا صاحب الشقة / إذا عندك شقة |
| نحن الأفضل في السوق | خلني أوريك الأرقام وأنت احكم |

**Delivery:**
- The presenter learns the **points**, not the words. Film each block separately (hook, step 1, step 2, …) and cut together. Never read the whole script from a teleprompter.
- Energy: calm confidence, like a friend who knows the numbers. Not a salesman shouting.
- Wear a thobe (and shemagh on some videos): it signals a local, trusted operator. Casual smart for behind-the-scenes days.

## 8. Comment-keyword funnel (plugs into your TikTok reply tool)

```
Video (keyword CTA)
   └─> Owner comments keyword ("تم")
         └─> Your tool posts ONE public reply (≤150 chars) → "check your DMs"
               └─> Owner messages @suitiee.sa → TikTok native keyword auto-reply sends details
                     └─> WhatsApp / call booked → Unit assessment → Signed
```

### Keyword strategy
You said the keyword can be any word, sometimes **"تم"**. That works, with one caution:

> ⚠️ **"تم" is a very common word in Arabic comments** ("تم المتابعة", "تم الإرسال", "تم 👍"). If the tool does a *contains* match, it will reply to people who aren't leads. Recommendation:
> - Configure the tool to match when the **whole comment** equals the keyword (after trimming spaces/emoji), **or**
> - Use a **distinct keyword per video** (list below). This also tells you exactly which video produced each lead, which matters most when you pick ad winners.

| Video | Keyword |
|---|---|
| V1, V14 | دخل |
| V2 | أمان |
| V3, V13 | تم |
| V4, V11 | قنوات |
| V5 | تجربة |
| V6 | سعر |
| V7, V10 | شقتي |
| V8 | تقرير |
| V9 | جديدة |
| V12 | (reply video, reuse the keyword of the original comment) |

**Operational rule (matches the tool's safeguards):** switch the reply rule **ON before publishing** each video. The tool only answers comments posted after the rule is on, so a late switch loses the first-hour comments, which are the most valuable.

### Public reply variants (rotate; all < 150 characters)
Rotating 4–5 versions stops the replies looking like spam, which both users and TikTok notice.
1. `أرسلنا لك التفاصيل، شيّك على رسائلك 📩 وإذا ما وصلت راسلنا مباشرة`
2. `حياك الله! كلمنا على الخاص وبنرسل لك كل التفاصيل عن إدارة شقتك 🔑`
3. `تمام 👌 افتح الرسائل وأرسل لنا "شقتي" ونكمل معك هناك`
4. `وصلنا! راسلنا بالخاص ونحسب لك دخل شقتك المتوقع 📊`
5. `أبشر، التفاصيل بالخاص 📩 راسلنا ونرد عليك بأسرع وقت`

### TikTok native DM auto-reply (Business Suite)
> هلا والله 👋 شكراً لتواصلك مع سويتي.
> عشان نحسب لك الدخل المتوقع لشقتك، أرسل لنا:
> ١) الحي ٢) عدد الغرف ٣) هل الشقة مفروشة؟
> أو كلمنا مباشرة واتساب: {{whatsapp_link}}
> تقدر تشوف أعمالنا هنا: suitiee.com

### Instagram equivalent
Your tool is TikTok-only. On Instagram, use Meta's built-in **automated responses / comment-to-DM** (or ManyChat) with the same keywords and the same DM text, so the experience is the same on both platforms.

## 12. Paid amplification (baseline 6,000 SAR, range 3–10k)

### Principle
**Don't make ads, promote winners.** Organic tells us what works for free, and money only goes behind proven creative.

### Phases
| Phase | Days | Spend | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Setup | 1–3 | 0 | TikTok Pixel + Meta Pixel on suitiee.com, lead form, WhatsApp Business link, Spark Ads authorization codes on every organic post |
| 1. Organic test | 1–14 | 0 | Post V1–V7, measure hook rate, watch %, keyword comments |
| 2. Creative test | 10–20 | ~2,000 SAR | Top 3 videos × 3 hooks each = 9 ads, ~220 SAR each |
| 3. Scale | 18–30 | ~3,500 SAR | Put budget behind the 2 best ads by **cost per qualified lead** |
| 4. Retarget | 18–30 | ~500 SAR | Retarget 50%+ video viewers and profile visitors with V5 (testimonial) + V8 (report) |

### Split and setup
| | TikTok (≈55%) | Meta: IG Reels + FB (≈45%) |
|---|---|---|
| Format | **Spark Ads** (keeps likes/comments/profile) | Boost the organic Reel / use the post ID in Ads Manager |
| Objective | Lead generation (instant form) **or** Messages → WhatsApp | **Click-to-WhatsApp** (very strong in KSA) or Leads (instant form) |
| Location | Riyadh (+ optional: Saudis living in Jeddah/Eastern Province who own units in Riyadh) | same |
| Age | 27–60 | 27–60 |
| Targeting | Broad + interests: real estate, investment, Airbnb | Broad (Advantage+). Let the creative do the targeting |
| CTA | "اكتب لنا" / "Contact us" | "Send WhatsApp message" |

**Lead form questions (qualifies owners):** الحي · عدد الغرف · الشقة حالياً (فاضية / مؤجرة سنوي / يومي) · مفروشة؟ · رقم الجوال

### Kill / scale rules (check every 48h)
- 3s hook rate < 25% after 2,000 impressions → **kill the hook**, keep the body, test a new hook.
- CPL > 2× target after 150 SAR spend → **pause**.
- CPL ≤ target for 3 days → **+20% budget** every 48h (not more, or the learning phase resets).
- Frequency > 3 on retargeting → rotate the creative.

### Compliance check (KSA)
- Numbers in ads must match the proof sheet and be defensible.
- If the presenter is **not** a Suitiee employee, check whether they need a **Mawthooq** (GCAM advertiser) license before you run paid ads with their face.
- Licensing claims (`{{licensing}}`) only if true.

## 13. Weekly review ritual (30 minutes, every Saturday)

1. Pull per video: views, 3s hold %, avg watch %, shares, saves, **keyword comments**, DMs, leads, signed.
2. Rank by **leads per 1,000 views**, not views.
3. Decide:
   - 🏆 Winner (top 2): make a **remix** (same body, new hook) for next week + nominate it for ads.
   - 🔧 Good body, weak hook (watch % fine, 3s hold low): re-cut with an alt hook.
   - ❌ Weak all round: drop the angle.
4. Read the comments. The best questions become V12-style reply videos and next month's scripts.

## 15. What makes it look "odd" (avoid list)

- Reading from a script, eyes moving left-right → film in blocks, learn the points.
- Formal MSA or translated phrasing → see the language table in §7.
- Stock "corporate" music and logo intros → cold open, voice first.
- Too many claims in one video → **one video = one idea**.
- The same public reply under every comment → rotate the 5 variants.
- "رابط في البايو" as the only CTA → the keyword comment is lower friction and feeds the algorithm.
- Over-polished ad look on organic → organic stays native (phone-shot); polish only the paid cut if testing shows it helps.
