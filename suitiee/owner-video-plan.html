02Who we're talking to

A and B convert fastest, so 70% of the calendar targets them.

A · The annual-lease owner

Unit rented yearly, income flat. "My income hasn't moved in years." Needs the money math: annual vs short-term.

B · The DIY host

Already on Airbnb / Gathern / Booking, runs it alone. 3am messages, no-show cleaners, flat pricing. Needs "keep the income, drop the headache", via full management or channel manager only.

C · The new-unit owner

Just received a unit (often north Riyadh). Unsure whether to rent it yearly, rent it short-term or sell. Needs a clear first step.

D · The multi-unit investor

3+ units, spreadsheets. Needs reporting, occupancy numbers and hands-off operations.

05Algorithm playbook

What both platforms reward

  1. The first 1–2 seconds. Visual hook, on-screen text and spoken hook all start at frame 1. No logo intro and no "السلام عليكم، معكم…".
  2. Watch time. Aim for 30–50 seconds and cut every pause (jump cuts every 2–4s).
  3. Rewatches. End on a line that loops back to the opening, or on a number that makes people scroll back.
  4. Comments. The keyword CTA is also a ranking signal, because every تم counts as a comment.
  5. Shares and sends. On Instagram, sends per reach is one of the strongest signals. Give owners a reason to forward it: "أرسلها لأخوك اللي عنده شقة".
  6. Search. TikTok works as a search engine in KSA. Say the keywords out loud (auto-captions are indexed) and put them on screen and in the caption: إدارة شقق الرياض، تأجير يومي، جاذر إن، إير بي إن بي، دخل الشقة، إيجار سنوي.

TikTok

Post natively. Put a trending low-volume sound under the voice at 10–15%. Ad cuts use the Commercial Music Library only. Use 3–5 hashtags: #الرياض #تأجير_يومي #إدارة_أملاك #سويتي. Pin a keyword comment on every video. Reply to comments with video. Don't delete low performers in the first 72h, because TikTok often picks videos up again days later.

Instagram Reels

Never upload the file with the TikTok watermark. Export a clean master. Test hooks with Trial Reels. Keep text out of the bottom 20% and the right 15%. Start the caption with the hook and the keyword CTA, then 3–5 hashtags. Share every Reel to Stories with a poll: "عندك شقة مؤجرة سنوي؟ نعم / لا".

Ad-ready rules for every shoot

  • Film 3 alternative hooks per video (same body). These become the A/B/C ad variants.
  • Film the CTA twice: "اكتب تم بالتعليقات" (organic) and "اضغط الزر تحت وكلمنا واتساب" (ads).
  • Keep 5–10 seconds of clean B-roll per video (apartment, keys, app screen).
  • Burn in Arabic subtitles in dialect, exactly as spoken, not in formal Arabic (MSA).

07Arabic guide: so it doesn't sound odd

The biggest risk is a script that sounds translated or read off a page.

Do

Talk like you're explaining it to a cousin at a jalsa: short sentences, one idea each. Use fillers sparingly (بصراحة، شوف، خلني أقولك، والله، يعني). Round numbers in speech ("قرابة ٧٠ ألف"). Say platform names the way people say them: إير بي إن بي، جاذر إن، بوكينق. Film in real places.

Delivery

The presenter learns the points, not the words. Film each block separately and cut them together, with no full-script teleprompter. Aim for calm confidence, like a friend who knows the numbers. Wear a thobe (and shemagh on some videos). Smart casual works for behind-the-scenes days.

08Comment-keyword funnel

Plugs into our TikTok reply tool. The tool posts one public reply, and TikTok's native keyword auto-reply sends the details by DM.

  1. Video with keyword CTA
  2. Owner comments keyword
  3. Tool posts 1 public reply (≤150 chars)
  4. Owner DMs → native auto-reply
  5. WhatsApp / call
  6. Unit assessment → signed
"تم" is a very common word in comments ("تم المتابعة"، "تم 👍"). If the tool matches any comment that contains it, it will reply to people who aren't leads. Either match only when the whole comment is the keyword, or use a different keyword per video (below). Per-video keywords also show which video brought each lead.

    Kill and scale rules (check every 48h)

    • Hook rate < 25% after 2,000 impressions → kill the hook, keep the body, test a new hook.
    • Cost per lead > 2× target after 150 SAR spend → pause.
    • Cost per lead ≤ target for 3 days → +20% budget every 48h. Bigger jumps restart the platform's learning phase.
    • Frequency > 3 on retargeting → rotate the creative.
    Compliance (KSA). Numbers in ads must match the proof sheet. If the presenter is not a Suitiee employee, check whether they need a Mawthooq advertiser license before their face runs in paid ads. Only mention licensing if licensing = yes.

    12Weekly review, every Saturday (30 min)

    1. Pull per video: views, 3s hold %, average watch %, shares, saves, keyword comments, DMs, leads, signed.
    2. Rank by leads per 1,000 views, not by views.
    3. Decide:
      • Winner (top 2): remix it (same body, new hook) for next week and nominate it for ads.
      • Good body, weak hook (watch % fine, 3s hold low): re-cut with an alt hook.
      • Weak all round: drop the angle.
    4. Read the comments. The best questions become reply videos (like V12) and next month's scripts.
